# Terminology

The words below are shared by this repository and the [widget](https://github.com/JessengBijleng/Qijk-widget). Use them consistently in code, docs and conversations.

## People and parties

- **Lead**: the person who uses the widget on a customer's website, usually someone who is considering having their kitchen wrapped. Uploads a photo and creates visualizations.
- **Customer**: the business that handles the kitchen wrapping (for example a kitchen wrapping company). Customers pay us to use the widget, embed it on their website and receive the leads' results.
- **Widget**: the frontend that customers embed in an iframe. This API's only client for now.
- **AI provider**: the external service (OpenAI, Gemini, ...) that analyses photos and generates visualizations. A paid dependency we call on the lead's behalf.

## Things

- **Wrap / kitchen wrap**: a foil or film applied over existing kitchen cabinets, doors and surfaces to change how they look without replacing them.
- **Analysis**: what the AI provider found in a lead's kitchen photo: the number of cabinets and drawers to wrap, an estimate of the wrappable surface and a description for the lead. Stored as a `KitchenAnalysis`.
- **Visualization**: the image that shows a lead's kitchen with one or more wraps applied. Generated by the AI provider through this API.
- **Embed**: the snippet (iframe) a customer places on their website to load the widget.
- **Request**: something a lead did through the widget that we store, such as uploading a photo or generating a visualization.
